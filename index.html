What is an Unauthorized Advertisement?

According to municipal guidelines and sections 328 and 328A of the Mumbai Municipal Corporation (MMC) Act, 1888, an outdoor display is classified as unauthorized and illegal if it meets any of the following conditions:

  • Absence of a Valid BMC License: Any commercial billboard, LED/LCD screen, glow sign, or structural frame erected without a formal permit issued by the BMC's License Department.
  • Missing Regulatory NOCs: Displays erected on private premises, housing society structures, or government lands (such as Railways, MHADA, or GRP) without obtaining both land-owner consent and mandatory municipal clearance.
  • Prohibited Locations: Any advertisement installed on footpaths, traffic islands, public road medians, or building terraces, all of which are strictly banned under the updated safety guidelines.
  • Failure of Structural & Safety Standards: Billboards exceeding the maximum permissible size cap (typically 40 ft x 40 ft), lacking a valid Structural Stability Certificate from a BMC-approved engineer, or operating without mandatory third-party insurance coverage.
  • Expired Permissions: Any media placement that remains active beyond the three-month grace window following the expiry of its official BMC display permit.
  • Traffic Hazards: Illuminated or digital OOH media operated without an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Joint Commissioner of Police (Traffic), or failing to comply with flickering and nighttime luminance caps.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Mumbai safer and aesthetically vibrant for every citizen. The sole purpose of Watch Mumbai is to assist municipal authorities and speed up action—not to revolt against or undermine the operations of the BMC by any means.
